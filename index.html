From Customer Call to Five-Star Follow-Up

Every step carries the same customer context forward, so your office and field team can keep moving without re-entering information.

  1. Customer callsA new or returning customer reaches your business.
  2. AI answersMyAIworker responds 24/7 in your business voice.
  3. QualifiesIt gathers the right details and identifies the service needed.
  4. Books the right employeeSkills, service area, and availability guide the appointment.
  5. Adds to their calendarThe appropriate employee receives the job on their calendar.
  6. Updates the CRMContact details, notes, and status stay organized.
  7. Sends confirmations & remindersAutomatic SMS and email reduce uncertainty and no-shows.
  8. Follows upThe conversation continues after the appointment.
  9. Requests a reviewHappy customers are invited to share their experience.
